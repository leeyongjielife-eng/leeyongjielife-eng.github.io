Knowledge Website

AI learning quickly became fragmented across too many formats and sources, so I built a personal knowledge website to collect only the material I want to keep. China Oven Export

AI market research · 2026

Situation. Export work was slowed by scattered research, unclear offers, and manual follow-up. Task. Build a repeatable workflow with clear roles, visible handoffs, and human review. Action. The system is in place and the outbound pipeline has reached 250+ external contacts.
